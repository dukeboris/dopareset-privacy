Frequently Asked Questions

Subscription & Billing

  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → DopaReset → Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • How does the 7-day free trial work? The yearly plan starts with a free 7-day trial. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you will not be charged.
  • I reinstalled the app / switched phones. How do I get my Pro back? Open DopaReset → Me → Subscription & Restore → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase.
  • My subscription renewed but the app shows it as expired. Tap Me → Subscription & Restore → Restore Purchases to refresh your status.
  • How do I request a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Your Data

  • Where is my data stored? Everything (check-ins, challenge progress, milestones, subscription status) is stored locally on your device only. We run no servers and collect nothing. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Will my data sync to a new phone? No. Because no data ever leaves your device, uninstalling the app or switching phones erases your progress. Your purchases can always be restored (see above).
  • How do I delete my data? Delete the app — that removes all local data completely. There is nothing to delete on our side, since we hold none of your data.

Reminders & Notifications

  • I'm not receiving reminders. Open iOS Settings → DopaReset → Notifications and make sure notifications are allowed. Then in the app, go to Me → Reminders, toggle reminders off and on, and tap "Send a test notification" — a test reminder should arrive within about a minute.
  • How do reminders work? Reminders are local notifications created on your device. Opening the app at least once a week keeps them scheduled.

About the App

  • What is DopaReset? A 24-hour dopamine detox challenge that scales to 7-day and 30-day programs: a morning pledge, an evening check-in, streaks and milestones, and a weekly report of the hours you win back from scrolling.
  • What happens if I relapse? Your total progress is never erased. The current streak restarts, but your lifetime clean days stay — every attempt counts.
