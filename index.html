DopaReset 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 9 月 2 日 · 适用于 iOS 应用 DopaReset: Dopamine Detox

我们的核心原则

你的数据只属于你，且只留在你的手机上。DopaReset 从设计上就是一个"纯本地"应用：我们没有服务器端账号系统，不收集、不上传、不分析你的任何个人数据。

我们收集什么数据

不收集任何个人信息。具体来说：

  • 你在引导流程中填写的回答（使用习惯、身份选择、个人宣言等）、打卡记录、挑战进度、里程碑与订阅状态，全部仅存储在你的设备本地（iOS 应用沙盒内），从不上传到任何服务器。
  • 我们不要求注册账号，不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号或任何身份信息。
  • 我们不使用任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK，不接入任何分析服务。
  • 应用不会访问你的通讯录、照片（除你主动使用"保存成就卡到相册"功能外）、位置、麦克风或摄像头。

应用内购买

订阅与购买由 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会接触、存储或处理你的支付信息（卡号、账单地址等）。购买记录与退款政策以 Apple 的条款为准。

本地通知

如果你开启提醒，应用仅在你的设备上创建本地定时通知（早间誓言与晚间日志提醒）。通知内容在设备上生成，不经过任何推送服务器。

数据的删除

卸载 DopaReset 即删除全部本地数据。应用内没有账号体系，因此不存在"注销账号"或"删除服务器数据"的问题——数据从未离开过你的设备。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，且我们不会有意收集任何儿童的数据（我们实际上不收集任何人的数据）。

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改顶部日期。任何涉及数据处理方式的变化都会在应用更新说明中明确告知。

DopaReset Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app DopaReset: Dopamine Detox

Our core principle

Your data belongs to you, and it never leaves your phone. DopaReset is local-only by design: we have no server-side account system, and we do not collect, upload, or analyze any of your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • Your onboarding answers, check-ins, challenge progress, milestones, and subscription status are stored only on your device (inside the iOS app sandbox) and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No account registration. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identity information.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No analytics services of any kind.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos (except when you explicitly use "Save achievement card to Photos"), location, microphone, or camera.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We never see, store, or process your payment information. Purchase records and refunds are governed by Apple's terms.

Local notifications

If you enable reminders, the app creates local scheduled notifications on your device only (morning pledge and evening journal reminders). Notification content is generated on-device and never passes through any push server.

Data deletion

Deleting DopaReset removes all of its data. Since there is no account system and no server-side storage, there is nothing to delete on our side — your data was never uploaded in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
